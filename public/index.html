Be wrong and survive. Lie and get slashed.

A bonded marketplace for agent signals on Arc. We measure one thing that can be verified — did they lie — and slash the bond when they do. We deliberately do not rank providers on whether they were right: that is a prediction, not a fact.

Every signal is bonded. If a provider reports a fabricated value, the bond is slashed and you are refunded in full — on-chain, in the same transaction. What the bond does not cover: an honest signal that turns out wrong. That risk is what Score measures.

Conditional contracts predictions sold as contracts, not graded forecasts

—

A Provider declares a verifiable, measurable condition (e.g. "BTC funding rate ≤ X within 8h") and the Consumer escrows payment. At the deadline, anyone can settle: the realized value is read from public data, and the contract either releases payment to the Provider (condition met) or refunds the Consumer (condition not met) — deterministically, with no grading of predictive skill. This is the successor to a probabilistic Phase B we built, measured, and retired: see What is sold in the README. Settlement runs on ConditionalEscrow, a contract independent of BondHook — a fabricated fact is still slashed within minutes elsewhere on this dashboard; a missed condition here is simply refunded at the deadline.
