Be wrong and survive. Lie and get slashed.

A bonded marketplace for agent signals on Arc. Reputation is two independent things — did they lie, and were they right — and we measure them separately.

Every signal is bonded. If a provider reports a fabricated value, the bond is slashed and you are refunded in full — on-chain, in the same transaction. What the bond does not cover: an honest signal that turns out wrong. That risk is what Score measures.

Reliability — did they lie?

Verified counts deliveries that passed instant Phase A checks against the CEX median. Purely deterministic, and resets to zero the moment a bond is slashed. The tier is just a band on that count.
